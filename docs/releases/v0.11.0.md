**TL;DR:** A new **Sweep** list at the top of the board shows which PRs need a human, why, and what to click next. Full refreshes are **about 3× faster**, and whole-org views on big orgs load instead of failing.

## ✨ New

- **Sweep list**: every stuck PR, with the reason and how long it's been stuck.
- **One-click next step** on each row: Fix CI, Resolve conflict, Re-review, Merge, Request reviewers, Nudge.
- **Snooze** a row until the PR changes, gets worse, or tomorrow.
- **Quiet rows**: PRs that are only waiting or stale, or untouched for a month, sit behind **Show quiet**.
- **Sprint-end warning** in a sprint's last 2 days: "N open PRs aren't approved yet."
- **Tray menu** counts what needs the team's attention.
- **Open means open**: PRs carried over from before your date range now show up.
- **Team review requests** show in the Awaiting column, like `acme/platform`.

## ⚡ Faster

- Full refresh on a 5-person team: **28.9 s → 9.1 s**.
- Merged PRs load a week at a time, 4 weeks in parallel.

## 🐛 Fixed

- Whole-org views on big orgs no longer fail with `HTTP 502`.
- A cut-off response from GitHub is retried instead of breaking the refresh.

## 👀 Heads up

- **First refresh after updating is a full one**: the cache format changed. A few seconds longer, once.
- **Counts may go up**: open PRs from before your date range are included now.
- **Whole-org views on very large orgs are still slow** (a couple of minutes): GitHub rate-limits the searches.
- **The sprint-end warning needs a To date** in the header.

## ⬆️ Getting it

Installed copies update themselves. When the header says the update is ready, click **Restart**.
