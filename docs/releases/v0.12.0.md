**TL;DR:** PR Sweep has a **calmer, clearer new look**, and it **knows your sprints**: set the schedule once, and the board opens on the current sprint and moves on by itself.

## New

- **Sprints**: set when the first one starts and how long they run. The board opens on the current sprint.
- **Rolls over by itself**: when a sprint ends, the board moves to the next one. No list to keep up to date.
- **Sprint arrows** in the top bar step back and forward. **Current** jumps back to today's sprint.
- **Rename a sprint or make one longer** in Settings. The sprints after it move to follow.
- **Custom** still works: pick it in the top bar and set From and To dates, like before.
- **New look** in light and dark: easier-to-read tables, a row of counts at the top, and status you can read without relying on color.
- **Health strip** at the top: each count says whether it's fine, like "3 untouched for 5+ days".
- **CI status in words**: Pass, Fail or Running on every open PR.
- **Freshness** in the top bar: when the board last updated, or that the last refresh failed.
- **Clear filters** in one click when author chips or search hide everything.

## Fixed

- **Ctrl+R** reloads the board instead of leaving a blank window.
- **Error messages** say what went wrong in plain words, with GitHub's details underneath.

## Heads up

- **Your saved date range stays as it is** until you set up sprints in Settings.
- **The sprint-end warning** works with sprints on their own. With a custom range, it still needs a To date.
- **Snoozed and quiet rows** now show a tag instead of being faded out.

## Getting it

Installed copies update themselves. When the top bar says **Restart to update**, click it.
