**PR Sweep 0.13.1: CI status that keeps up.** A check that finishes or is re-run now shows on the next auto-refresh, with no Refresh needed.

## Try it

1. Leave PR Sweep running in the tray.
2. Push to an open PR and let its checks run, even past 10 minutes.
3. Within 5 minutes of them finishing, the CI column and the Sweep show the result.

## What's fixed

- **Long CI runs:** checks that took more than 5–10 minutes no longer stay "pending" until a manual Refresh.
- **Re-runs:** re-running a failed check now shows, instead of "CI failing" sticking in the Sweep and the standup.
- **Approved, not merged:** approved PRs whose checks passed late now show there.
- **Tray:** "CI failed" now notifies for checks that fail late, too.

## Heads-up

- **First refresh after updating is a full one:** the cache format changed, so it takes a few seconds longer, once.
- **Auto-refreshes re-check CI** on PRs still running or failing, which adds a fraction of a second.
