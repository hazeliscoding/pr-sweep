**TL;DR:** The **CI status stays current**: a check that finishes or is re-run now shows on the next auto-refresh, instead of waiting for a manual Refresh.

## Fixed

- **CI that ran longer than 5–10 minutes stayed pending** until you pressed Refresh. The next auto-refresh now picks up the result.
- **A re-run of a failed check now shows**: before, the PR kept saying "CI failing" in the Sweep and the standup.
- **Approved PRs whose checks passed late now show as "Approved, not merged"**, instead of waiting for CI that had already finished.
- **The tray's "CI failed" notification fires for checks that fail late**, too.

## Heads up

- **First refresh after updating is a full one**: the cache format changed. A few seconds longer, once.
- **Auto-refreshes also re-check CI on PRs still running or failing**, which adds a fraction of a second.

## Getting it

Installed copies update themselves. When the top bar says **Restart to update**, click it. Portable copies show **Download v0.13.1** in the top bar.
